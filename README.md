# CCOI-Interoperability
Collaborative Community for Ophthalmic Innovation (CCOI) Interoperability Work Group

[CCOI](https://www.cc-oi.org/) is the Collaborative Community on Ophthalmic Innovation, a nonprofit that brings together academia, industry, regulators, clinicians and patient advocates to speed up ophthalmic medical product development. Interoperability is one of many CCOI workstreams. CCOI has grown from 6 to 17 working groups, spanning AI and interoperability as well as disease-specific areas like glaucoma, AMD, diabetic retinopathy, and ocular oncology.

Who’s involved and the goal. This is an international community drawn from device manufacturing, academic research, pharma, and EMR/EHR vendors, aiming to improve standards and interoperability between eye care products. We believe that better interoperability will get products to market more efficiently and open up new opportunities to improve healthcare through the eye. We also have direct interaction and member overlap with the DICOM WG9 and the [OHDSI Eye Care and Vison Work Group](https://ohdsi.org).
